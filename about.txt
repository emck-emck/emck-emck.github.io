    This website exists as a means of self-expression and as a showcase of my tech projects from over the years. I welcome all visitors (try your hand at Portal Golf while you're here ^.^).

    I have a deep passion for many sorts of creative projects. I've made small video games, explored music theory, built scripts to solve obscure math problems, drawn cover art for my paper journals; the list goes on.

    I consider myself a creative and analytical person with an interest in complex problem solving. I once theorized that Determinism is something that can be calculated, then took up the task by myself. Of course I didn't get too far, but reaching for the abstract stars is something I commonly do.

    What I'm saying is, I don't make every shot that I take, but that may be a result of aiming as high as I possibly can.

    An example of success would be how I switched my career from retail to to take an Electrical Engineering Technologist diploma and I made honour role 4 semesters in a row (and counting). As happy as I am with that success, I feel I was unchallenged by it. I saw people try and fail, but that doesn't make me feel better; downward comparisons are the death of self-growth.

    Therefore, I seek a new challenge. Once I've graduated from college, I hope to find ways to keep growing as a person while connecting with new people and making the world a better place.
